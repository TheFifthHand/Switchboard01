OMNI SONG - START HERE
======================

Omni Song lets you make electronic music in your web browser, on your own
computer. Press one button and a beat plays; then make it yours. It needs no
account and no internet connection once it is on your computer. Sound is
made on your device.

Omni Song is version 2.0 of the app that was called SWITCHBOARD / 01. Your
projects and settings carry over (see 4 and 6).

Recommended browser: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (current versions).
Use headphones or speakers; start with a moderate volume.


1. OPEN IT (Windows, no install needed)
---------------------------------------
  1. Extract this whole zip to a folder (right-click > Extract All...).
     Double-clicking inside the zip without extracting will NOT work.
  2. Double-click  "Start Omni Song.bat".
     A small black window opens and your browser shows Omni Song at
     http://127.0.0.1:4173/
     If Windows SmartScreen asks, choose "More info" > "Run anyway".
     The window uses PowerShell, which is built into Windows 10 and 11.
  3. Keep that small window open while you play.

  To STOP: close the small black window (or press Ctrl+C in it).
  The launcher only serves the "app" folder, and only to this computer
  (loopback address 127.0.0.1). Nothing is uploaded.

  Already running? Double-clicking "Start Omni Song.bat" again just opens
  the running copy in your browser; the new window closes by itself after a
  few seconds. If the open window runs another version (for example the
  one you used before this download), the new window says so and waits:
  close the other small black window, and the new one starts this version
  by itself, at the same address, with all your projects. Open pages switch
  to the new version by themselves; a page you are using offers "Update"
  in the More menu instead, so nothing is interrupted.

  Your browser keeps projects separately for each address, and the port
  number (4173) is part of the address. The launcher always uses
  http://127.0.0.1:4173/ unless another program is using that port; then
  it explains what that means before it opens anywhere else (see 5).


2. OTHER WAYS TO OPEN IT (any system)
-------------------------------------
  With Node.js 18+ installed:
      node launcher/serve.mjs app
  With Python 3 installed:
      python -m http.server 4173 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory app
  then open http://127.0.0.1:4173/ in Chrome or Edge. Keep port 4173 so
  you find the same projects every time.

  Why not just double-click app/index.html?  Browsers block the module
  loading, storage and offline features the app needs on file:// pages,
  so it must be served from a (local) web address.


3. JUMP IN
----------
  Click "Jump In". A song starts playing at once - this click is what
  allows the browser to make sound. It repeats its main section until
  you press Stop. Or click "Make a beat from scratch" for an empty drum
  loop that plays while you click squares into it.

  Omni Song opens on one screen: your song.
    * Each row is an instrument (Drums, Bass, Keys & chords, Melody ...).
      Each block on a row is a loop: a short piece of music that repeats.
    * Play (or the Space bar) plays and pauses your song. Stop
      (Shift+Space) goes back to the start. Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes back
      anything you do.
    * Click a loop to change it: it opens under the song. "Draw" shows
      its squares or notes (click to add or take away). "Play" turns it
      into big squares to play with the mouse or the computer keys, and
      "Record into this loop" keeps what you play. Only notes that fit
      your song are offered; the home note has a little house on it.
    * "Try a ready-made beat..." or "Try a ready-made loop..." puts one
      in that fits your song. Try a few: Undo goes back.
    * Click an instrument's name (left of its row) for its sound:
      "Change sound..." picks another one, and the big knobs shape it.
    * "+ Add instrument" adds a bass, keys & chords, a melody and more,
      each with a loop that fits. "Loops" on the right has many more:
      press the play arrow to hear one, then drag it onto an instrument.
    * "Make it longer" adds an intro, an ending or a new section made
      from your own music; "Make it shorter" (same menu) takes a
      section out.
    * "Record" (at the top) plays along into the song from where it is.
    * "Share" (top right) saves a music file to send to anyone (a WAV,
      optionally as loud as streaming songs - only the file changes), or
      the project to keep working on (an .omnisong.zip file).
    * The line at the bottom says one next thing to try. "Hide tips"
      turns it off; the More menu (the button with three dots, top
      right) has "Tips" to bring it back.
    * Press ? (or More > Help...) for "How it works" on one page.
    * The More menu also holds the Jam pads (play loops live), the
      Mixer, every setting of a sound, Mute all sound, and MIDI & audio
      for a MIDI keyboard or a microphone.
    * More > "Show every control" switches to Advanced: the Play, Shape,
      Song and Mix tabs with every control, as in version 2.4.
      Switching never changes your music. Point at any control to see
      what it does.
    * Knobs: drag up or down (hold Shift for fine steps). Double-click
      puts a knob back to its default. Arrow keys work too.
    * New in 3.0: one song screen for everyone (no tabs to learn), click
      a loop to change it, a Sound panel beside the song, "+ Add
      instrument", "Make it longer", tips that follow your song, and
      Share for a music file or the project. Everything from 2.4 is
      still there in Advanced.
    * Earlier versions added: make a beat from scratch and 164 loops in
      your song's key (2.4), songs with a row per instrument like
      GarageBand (2.3), chord pads and up to 8 scenes (2.2), and MIDI
      keyboards and audio input (2.0). Songs made with earlier versions
      open as they were.


4. YOUR WORK
------------
  Projects save automatically in this browser (look for "Saved" at the
  top right; an untouched starter shows "Preview" until your first
  change). Browser storage is working storage: for a real backup, click
  the folder button with your project's name (top right, or More >
  "Projects...") to open the Project library, then press
  "Export this project". It saves one .omnisong.zip file. To open that
  file on any computer, press "Import project file..." in the same
  Project library. Project files saved by SWITCHBOARD / 01 (.sb01.zip)
  open the same way.

  After the first visit the app also works offline in the same browser,
  at the same address. You can install it as an app from the browser's
  address bar (optional). If you installed SWITCHBOARD / 01 as an app, it
  updates itself to Omni Song: press "Update" when the app offers it.


5. TROUBLESHOOTING
------------------
  * No sound: check the system volume and output device; press the
    "Resume audio" button if the app shows one; make sure Mute All is off.
  * The black window says port 4173 is used by another program: it waits
    for you to press Enter, then opens Omni Song at another address
    (for example http://127.0.0.1:4174/). There, My projects starts
    empty. Your projects are not lost: they stay with
    http://127.0.0.1:4173/. To get back to them, close the black window,
    close the other program (or restart the computer) and start
    Omni Song again. To find the other program, open Command Prompt and
    run   netstat -ano | findstr :4173   - the last number is its PID,
    which Task Manager shows on the Details tab. To move a project from
    one address to the other, use "Export this project" and
    "Import project file...".
  * My projects looks empty: check that the address bar shows
    http://127.0.0.1:4173/. Projects belong to the browser and the
    address they were saved at; another browser, or a private window,
    has its own separate storage.
  * The window closes immediately: open PowerShell in this folder (in
    File Explorer, click the address bar, type  powershell  and press
    Enter) and run
        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File launcher\serve.ps1
    to see the error message.


6. COMING FROM SWITCHBOARD / 01
-------------------------------
  * "Start SWITCHBOARD.bat" is now called "Start Omni Song.bat". It works
    the same way and opens the same address, http://127.0.0.1:4173/, so
    every project you saved in SWITCHBOARD / 01 is still in My projects.
    If you kept a shortcut to the old .bat file, point it at the new one
    (or extract this version over the old folder and delete the old .bat).
  * Project files: new ones end in .omnisong.zip; your .sb01.zip files
    still import.

-------------------------
