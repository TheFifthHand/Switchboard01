# Asset provenance — SWITCHBOARD / 01

Every sound in SWITCHBOARD / 01 is generated on the user's device by original code in this
repository. No recorded samples, loops, presets or patterns from third parties are bundled.

| Asset | Source | Licence / permission |
|-------|--------|----------------------|
| Drum kits (15 kits × 16 voices: Round Machine, Tight Circuit, Dust Tape, Bright Steel, Hand Percussion, 808 Machine, 909 Punch, Trap Night, Lo-fi Crate, Studio Acoustic, Electro Wire, Iron Forge, Minimal Click, Breakbeat Crate, Afro Latin) | Synthesized at runtime by `src/audio/instruments/drumSynth.ts` from recipes in `src/audio/instruments/kits.ts`. Kits named after a style or a classic machine ("808", "909") are original recipes in that general style; no recordings, samples or circuit models of those machines are used | Original work of this project |
